Good to know

How long is an appointment?

Brow and face waxing appointments are 45 minutes. Lash services are 1½ hours.

Do I need a deposit?

No. You pay in person at your appointment.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. No-shows are charged 50% of the booked service.

Do you take walk-ins?

Highbrow is by appointment only. Book online or text Kristin.

How often should I come in?

Many clients come monthly. Kristin will recommend what works for your brows.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, for any service. Text Kristin at 916-276-3296 to get one.

What services do you offer?

Brows, lash lifts, and face waxing: everything above the shoulders.
